## Calculus  Notes

### the main idea is:
    You can undertand complicated things by breaking them into extermely small pieces, then adding those pieces together.

    Big object/area
    ↓
    Break it into tiny pieces
    ↓
    Each piece uses something like \(dx\)
    ↓
    Add all the tiny pieces
    ↓
    Get the total

    dx = a tiny change/interval in \(x\)
    Tiny pieces can be added together to find a whole.
    This leads to the integral.
    Calculus is about understanding tiny changes and how they build into something bigger.
